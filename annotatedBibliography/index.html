Annotated Bibliography

Computer Vision and Music

"Hand Landmarker." Google MediaPipe Solutions, Google, 2024. developers.google.com.

This site provides documentation that explains how Google's MediaPipe Hand Landmarker tracks 21 points on each hand through a live webcam and how to implement it in JavaScript. This is relevant to my project because it's the main library I'll be using to develop and build out the air piano feature. It includes live demos and code samples that allow you to test hand tracking in your browser. The documentation is maintained by Google's engineering team, so the information is reliable and consistently updated. Additionally, the model runs client-side with no server required.

AI and Music Learning

"Piano M + Airwave: An Intelligent Piano Learning System Powered by ROLI." ROLI, 2025. roli.com.

This page introduces ROLI's Piano System with Airwave, which is a product that uses computer vision and spatial AI to help users learn to play the piano. It tracks 27 joints in each hand at 90 frames per second and provides real-time feedback on techniques like posture, rhythm, and dynamics. It's relevant to my project because it also shows what the future for piano playing could look like. This product stands out because it combines hand tracking with an actual physical keyboard, which feels like a crossover between a traditional piano and how my air piano will function.

Music Education Equity

Chatwin, Fiona. "The Importance of Equitable Learning: Making Music Learning Accessible." Villa Musica, 23 Dec. 2024. villamusica.org.

This article from Villa Musica, a nonprofit community music center in San Diego, explains why equitable access to music education matters and how financial barriers prevent many kids from having the opportunity to learn an instrument. It's relevant to my project because my project considers the idea of what happens when technology could completely remove that cost barrier. Considering the information from this organization was important because they're working on this problem through adjusting tuition based on what families can afford and offering free community programs, so their perspective is backed by real experiences. Villa Musica argues that musical education builds cognitive skills, emotional development, and community, but only if it's accessible, which reinforced my belief that the speculative questions in my project are worth asking users.
